02
DATA · SUSTAINABILITY · TECHNOLOGY

Environmental Data Project

I started this project after noticing how much environmental information about Kathmandu — including air quality, river health, waste, and related issues — exists, but is often scattered across reports, PDFs, and datasets that aren't easy for an ordinary person to explore.

I'm working on turning that information into something simpler: a format where patterns are visible, questions are easier to ask, and environmental information can become something people can actually use.

Current status:
Currently in development as I explore how environmental data can be organized and presented in a clear, understandable format.
The question:
How can environmental data become something people can actually use rather than something they simply read about?
What I'd do next:
Develop clearer visualizations and an easier way for people to explore the information themselves.
04 — LEADERSHIP

Model United Nations

Four years of research, public speaking, negotiation, journalism, and committee work.

MUN taught me that good ideas rarely arrive fully formed. They get questioned, negotiated, rewritten, and sometimes completely changed before they become something a group can agree on.

2022
Vice Chair · UNFCCC Committee

Facilitating discussion

As Vice Chair, I helped facilitate committee discussions among 20+ delegates exploring climate policy, sustainable development, and youth climate action.

The experience taught me that leadership isn't always about having the strongest argument in the room. Sometimes it's about recognizing where different arguments overlap and helping people build from there.

Facilitation · Negotiation · Public Speaking · Research
2023
Delegate · Commission on the Status of Women

Research & representation

Representing an assigned country meant researching its position on gender policy issues and then defending that perspective during committee discussions and negotiations.

It pushed me to separate what I personally believed from what I could actually argue with evidence.

MUN taught me something I still use outside the committee room: understanding a position doesn't require agreeing with it.

Research · Diplomacy · Negotiation · Communication
2019
Logistics Team

Conference coordination

Supported conference organization through group assignments, communication, and event coordination.

It was an early introduction to the work that happens behind the scenes to make a large event function — coordinating people, solving practical problems, and making sure different pieces come together.
